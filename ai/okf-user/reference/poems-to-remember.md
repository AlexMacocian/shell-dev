## The Tuft of Flowers

- **Author:** Robert Frost
- **Remember for:** A poem about a mower discovering a tuft of flowers left uncut, and recognizing a bond with the fellow worker who spared them. Its central feeling is that people can remain connected through their work and choices even when separated by time or circumstance.
